You land in the lobby after joining. From here you choose your game mode, meet friends and collect your streak rewards. There are several lobbies. The Nether star in your hotbar switches between them.

## Commands

- `/lobby`, `/l` or `/hub` takes you to a lobby from anywhere.
- `/spawn` takes you to the lobby spawn.
- `/leavequeue` leaves a queue.

## Return

When you come back to the lobby, you land at your last position or at spawn. Set this under `/settings lobby`. On your next login you return to your last mode or to the lobby. Set this under `/settings network`.

## Lobby settings

Under `/settings lobby` you choose:

- which players you see
- where you land when you return
- whether you double jump
- who may pick you up
