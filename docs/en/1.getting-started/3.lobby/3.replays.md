`/replays` opens **My recordings**. Start a replay from the lobby with **Watch in game**. `/replays cancel` cancels the start.

## Controls

While watching, `/replay` opens the controls:

- `/replay pause` pauses.
- `/replay resume` continues.
- `/replay speed <value>` changes the speed.
- `/replay free` switches to a free camera.
- `/replay follow` shows a player's view.
- `/replay end` ends the replay.

Afterwards you are back in the lobby.
