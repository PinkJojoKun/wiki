## Your account in game

`/account` opens your account. In the lobby you can also click your head in the hotbar.

- Profile
- Statistics
- Friends
- Clan
- Party
- Requests
- Messages
- Blocked
- Settings

You can open single pages directly, for example with `/account friends` or `/account messages`.

## Profile

`/profile` opens your profile: level, balances, rank, play time and first join. `/profile <player>` opens the profile of friends, party and clan members, blocked players and open requests.

## Messages

`/account messages` holds messages waiting for you:

- messages someone left while you were offline
- answers to your clan applications
- changes to your clan role
- team replies to your bug reports

You can mark them as read and delete them.

## Levels and XP

You collect XP while you play actively. There are two levels:

- **Network level** for the whole network
- **SMP level** in the SMP

Your profile shows how much XP you need for the next level. `/gz` congratulates others on their level.

## Currencies

- **Argonit:** the network currency
- **Silver:** the SMP currency

Your balance is shown in your account and in the sidebar.

## Streak

`/streak` opens your streak calendar. If you play actively enough on a day, it counts towards your streak. Collect rewards with **Collect rewards**.

**Ice** protects your streak on a missed day. You get one Ice for every third completed day.

## Inventory

`/inventory` opens your inventory with network and SMP boosters. A booster works for everyone. If one is already running, yours waits in the queue.

## Discord

Link your account with Discord. The SMP requires it.

1. Join the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).
2. Type `/minecraft link` there. You get a private code.
3. In game, type `/discord link <code>`.

`/discord` shows your status. `/discord unlink` removes the link. Afterwards it takes a while before you can link again.

## Website

`/web` gives you a personal sign-in link for [sirargon.net](https://sirargon.net). It works once and only for a short time. Do not share it. `/web logout-all` signs you out everywhere on the website.

On the website you find your statistics, your bug reports and the data export.

## Twitch

`/twitch link <code>` connects your account with the Twitch extension. You find the code in the extension. `/twitch unlink` removes the connection.

## Your data

`/exportdata` requests an export of your data. You find the download in your account on the website.

## Skin

`/skin` sets your skin for your next login: `original`, `steve`, `alex` or `backward_steve`.
