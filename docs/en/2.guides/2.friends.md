`/friends` opens your friends. You see who is online, AFK or offline, and on which server.

## Commands

- `/friends add <player>` sends a request.
- `/friends requests` shows open requests.
- `/friends accept <player>` accepts a request.
- `/friends decline <player>` declines it.
- `/friends cancel <player>` withdraws your request.
- `/friends remove <player>` ends a friendship.
- `/friends list` shows your friends.
- `/friends join <player>` follows a friend to their server.
- `/friends invite <player>` invites a friend to your party.
- `/friends help` shows all actions.

`/friend` works the same way.

## Follow a friend

You can follow a friend if they allow it, their online status is visible and their server has room. Whether others may follow you is set under `/settings privacy`.

## Who may send you requests

Under `/settings requests` you choose who may send you friend requests: everyone, friends of friends or nobody.

Playing together? Create a [party](/guides/party).
