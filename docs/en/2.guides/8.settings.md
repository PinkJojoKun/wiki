`/settings` opens your settings. You can open single pages directly, for example with `/settings privacy`.

## Chat

`/settings chat`

- **Name pings:** a sound when someone writes your name
- **Direct messages:** your consent, see [Messages](/guides/messages)
- **Join and leave:** all, selected or no messages
- **AFK messages:** all, selected or no messages
- **Do not announce levels:** others do not see your level-ups

“Selected” means your friends, your party and your clan.

## Requests

`/settings requests`

- **Messages from:** everyone, friends or nobody
- **Reply window:** how long someone you wrote to may reply
- **Friend requests:** everyone, friends of friends or nobody
- **Party invitations:** everyone, friends or nobody
- **Clan invitations:** everyone, friends or nobody

## Privacy

`/settings privacy`

- **Statistics:** public, friends only or only you, also on the website
- **Online status visible**
- **Server visible**
- **Last online visible**
- **Play time visible**
- **First join visible**
- **Allow following:** friends may follow you to your server

## Lobby

`/settings lobby`

- **Lobby players:** all, selected or none
- **Lobby return:** back to your last position or to spawn
- **Double jump:** on or off
- **Pick up:** who may pick you up: everyone, friends or nobody

## Network

`/settings network`

- **Reconnect to:** on your next login, back to your last mode or to the lobby

## Language

`/settings language`

Choose **Deutsch** or **English**. Or use your Minecraft client's language.

## Reset

**Reset all** sets every setting back to default. Your consent to direct messages stays.
