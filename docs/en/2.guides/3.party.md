`/party` opens your party. A party keeps your group together.

## Commands

- `/party create` creates a party.
- `/party invite <player>` invites and creates a party if needed.
- `/party accept <player>` accepts an invitation.
- `/party decline <player>` declines it.
- `/party cancel <player>` withdraws an invitation.
- `/party list` shows the members.
- `/party join <player>` follows a member.
- `/party leave` leaves the party.
- `/party help` shows all actions.

`/parties` works the same way.

## Party leader

Only the party leader can:

- invite players: `/party invite <player>`
- remove members: `/party kick <player>`
- hand over the lead: `/party leader <player>`
- disband the party: `/party disband`

If only one member is left, the party disbands.

## Party chat

`/partychat <message>` or `/pc <message>` writes in the party chat. Without a message, party chat becomes your default chat. More under [Chat](/guides/chat).
