## Co-builders

- `/plot add <player>` lets someone build while you are online.
- `/plot trust <player>` lets someone build even while you are offline.
- `/plot remove <player>` takes the permissions away again.

## Bans

- `/plot deny <player>` bans someone from your plot.
- `/plot kick <player>` kicks someone off your plot.

## Flags

`/plot flag set <flag> <value>` changes your plot's settings, for example:

- time and weather
- greeting and farewell
- music
- whether visitors may use things
- redstone, water and lava
- growth, melting and decay

`/plot flag list` shows all flags.

## Appearance

`/plot set floor|wall|border` changes the floor, wall or border.

## Reset

- `/plot clear` resets your plot.
- `/plot delete` gives up your plot.
