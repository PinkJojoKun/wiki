## Get a plot

- `/plot auto` gives you the next free plot.
- `/plot claim` claims the plot you stand on.
- `/plot home` takes you to your plot.

Instead of `/plot` you can use `/p`.

## Other plots

- `/plot visit <player>` visits a player's plot.
- `/plot list` lists plots.
- `/plot info` shows plot information.
- `/plot middle` takes you to the middle of the plot.

## Rating

- `/plot like` likes a plot.
- `/plot dislike` dislikes it.
