## SMP XP and levels

You get SMP XP when you do things in the SMP:

- mine ores and stone
- cut down trees
- harvest crops
- defeat mobs
- take items out of a furnace
- dig
- play actively

Blocks you placed yourself do not count. AFK does not count. With enough XP your SMP level rises. From level 2 you get one skill point per level.

## Commands

- `/skills` shows your abilities, your mana and the switches.
- `/skills tree` opens the skill tree.
- `/skills toggle <ability>` turns an ability on or off.
- `/skills reset` resets all stars for silver. You keep your XP and skill points.

## Skill tree

In the skill tree you unlock stars. Every star costs one skill point. Many stars need a predecessor and some points in the same category.

Every [realm](/game-modes/smp/realms) has its own skill tree. Use the switch at the top of the skill tree to change between realms. A realm you have not entered yet shows the **Undiscovered** tile there.

## Abilities

These abilities belong to the Overworld skill tree.

**Mining**

- Vein miner: mines connected ores at once
- Tunnel borer: mines an area of stone
- Mining XP and mining mana

**Forestry**

- Treecapitator: fells the whole trunk
- Replant: plants a sapling again
- Leaf fall: the tree's leaves decay at once
- Forestry XP and forestry mana

**Farming**

- Harvest scythe: harvests an area with the hoe
- Reseeding: sows harvested crops again
- Farming XP and farming mana

**Building**

- Long arms: more reach in your own territories

**Exploration**

- Sprint boost: run faster for a moment
- Soft fall: less fall damage
- Deep diver: more air under water

**Combat**

- Mob expert: more damage against mobs
- Second wind: regeneration at low health
- Combat XP

**Community**

- Team aura: more SMP XP when you play together
- Campfire rest: regeneration at your campfire

**Spirit**

- Mana storage: more mana
- Mana flow: mana recharges faster

**Crafting**

- Smelting master: more SMP XP at the furnace

**Digging**

- Excavator: digs an area with the shovel
- Gravel sifter: gravel always drops flint
- Digging XP and digging mana

## Nether skill tree

You see the Nether skill tree once you have entered the Nether. You reach it through the switch at the top of the skill tree. It has its own branches:

- Nether Mining
- Fire Ward
- Nether Combat
- Bartering
- Soul Speed
- Nether Forestry

The Nether skill tree uses the same skill points as the Overworld skill tree. The more levels you reach, the more points you have to spend. The End will later get its own skill tree in the same way.

## Mana

Abilities for larger areas only trigger while sneaking and cost mana. Your mana is shown above the hotbar and recharges over time.

## Good to know

- Abilities are off while fighting players.
- Territory permissions apply to every single block.
- When your tool is almost broken, you only break single blocks. That way it does not break.
