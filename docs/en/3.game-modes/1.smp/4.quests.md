## Silver

Silver is the SMP currency. You need it for territories, trading, spawners and skills. Your silver is shown in the sidebar.

How you get silver:

- play actively
- complete quests
- reach milestones
- sell and trade, see [Trading](/game-modes/smp/trading)

AFK does not count.

## Quests

`/quests` shows daily and weekly quests. All players have the same ones.

- Daily quests arrive at midnight. There are easy, medium and hard ones.
- Weekly quests arrive on Monday.
- Each quest shows your progress and the reward.

### Kinds of quests

- cut wood
- harvest crops
- defeat mobs
- mine ores
- smelt items in a furnace
- discover new chunks and biomes
- play together with others
- deliver items

Some quests name a target of the day, for example a certain kind of wood.

### Delivering

1. Open `/quests`.
2. Choose **Select for delivery** on the quest.
3. Click matching items in your inventory. They are used up.

## Milestones

`/milestones` shows one-time milestones. They guide you through your start, for example your first camp, your first quest or your first skill point. Every milestone pays silver.
