The SMP has three realms: the **Overworld**, the **Nether** and the **End**. Each realm has its own quests, its own milestones and its own skill tree.

## Nether

We announce on the Discord and in game when the Nether opens. Then build a Nether portal and step through.

## Discovering a realm

You only see a realm's content once you have entered it. Until then, `/quests`, `/milestones` and the skill tree show a dimmed **Undiscovered** tile in its place.

Once you have been to the Nether for the first time, you get:

- your own [Nether quests](/game-modes/smp/quests#nether-quests), on top of your Overworld quests
- a separate group of [Nether milestones](/game-modes/smp/quests#milestones)
- the [Nether skill tree](/game-modes/smp/skills#nether-skill-tree)

## End

The End follows later in the same way. We will announce in good time when it opens.
