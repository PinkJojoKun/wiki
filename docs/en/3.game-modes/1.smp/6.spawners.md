## Spawner shop

`/spawner` opens the spawner shop. Click a spawner and confirm the purchase. New spawners become available as the world progresses.

## Placing

You place spawners:

- in your camp
- in a city with the **Place spawners** permission

It does not work in the wilderness.

## How they work

Spawners do not spawn mobs. They collect loot and XP in a storage. This only works while an active player is nearby. The menu shows roughly how much comes together per hour.

## Stack

You stack identical spawners in one place:

- Right-click with a spawner in your hand adds one.
- Sneak and right-click adds all from your hand.

Only the same type with the same upgrades stacks. A stack has a limit. When you look at it, the game shows how full it is.

## Menu

Right-click the stack to open the **Spawner stack** menu:

- **XP:** click collects XP up to the next level, shift-click collects all.
- **Loot:** take items and filter by item.
- **Destroy drops:** delete new drops of an item right away.
- **Looting:** buy an upgrade for the whole stack.

## Breaking

- Only the owner breaks a spawner.
- You need a pickaxe with Silk Touch.
- The storage must be empty.
- Normally you take one spawner, sneaking takes the whole stack.

Spawners cannot be traded.
