## Auction house

`/ah` opens the auction house. It has three tabs:

- **Offers:** everything others are selling
- **My offers:** what you are selling
- **Mailbox:** what you bought or got back

You can filter by category, search and sort by price or date.

### Selling

1. Hold the item in your main hand.
2. Type `/ah sell`.
3. Choose a price and a duration: 12 hours, 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days.
4. Check the preview with price, fee and the amount you receive.

If your price differs a lot from recent sales, the game warns you. You can withdraw an offer at any time.

### Buying

Click an offer and confirm. The item goes into your inventory or, if there is no room, into your mailbox.

## Buy orders

With a buy order you look for an item.

- `/orders` shows all buy orders.
- `/orders create` creates a buy order.

You set the material, quantity and total price. Optionally you require minimum enchantments or use an item in your hand as an exact template. Your silver is held in escrow until delivery.

Others deliver to your order from their main hand, also in parts. You can withdraw an order and get the remaining silver back.

## Trades

Trade directly with a player, without a fee.

1. Type `/trade <player>`. You must be in the same world, at most 8 blocks apart.
2. The other player accepts the request within 20 seconds.
3. Click stacks in your inventory to offer them. You can also offer silver.
4. Click **Ready**. Once both are ready, the trade completes after 3 seconds.

`/trade` opens a running trade.

## Mailbox

`/ah mailbox` opens your mailbox. Whatever has no room in your inventory waits there.

## Not tradeable

Some items cannot be traded, for example spawners.

Trading is paused during maintenance.
