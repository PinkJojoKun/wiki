Besides the vanilla advancements, the SMP has more advancements. You get almost all of them the normal way in survival. The additional [items](/game-modes/smp/items) and [enchantments](/game-modes/smp/enchantments) have their own pages.

## Overworld

You find them in the advancements menu, mostly under **Adventure** and **Husbandry**. Depending on your language you see the German or the English name.

### Sirargon

| Sunny Side Up | Sonnenseite oben | Eat a [fried egg](/game-modes/smp/items#fried-egg) |

### Not obtainable

Villagers do not trade on the SMP, see [Villagers](/game-modes/smp/villagers). That is why you cannot get advancements that need trades with cartographers or librarians, for example **Networking**, **Call for Adventure**, **Charter to All**, **Bookworm** and the librarians' books.
