`<player>`, `<clan>` and `<message>` are placeholders. Type the name or text without the angle brackets. `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- [General](/commands/general): help, lobby and getting around
- [Account](/commands/account): profile, settings and links
- [Community](/commands/community): friends, party, messages and chat
- [Clans](/commands/clans): everything about your clan
- [SMP](/commands/smp): territories, trading, quests and skills
- [Creative](/commands/creative): plots and WorldEdit
