## Help

- `/guide` or `/help`: open the guide
- `/rules`: show the rules
- `/wiki`: open the wiki
- `/changelog` or `/changes`: show changes
- `/confirm`: open a pending confirmation

## Reporting

- `/bug`: report a bug
- `/bugs`: your bug reports
- `/report <player>`: report a player

## Getting around

- `/lobby`, `/l` or `/hub`: go to the lobby
- `/spawn`: go to the lobby spawn
- `/leavequeue`: leave a queue
- `/afk`: mark yourself as AFK

## Replays

- `/replays`: your recordings
- `/replays cancel`: cancel the start
- `/replay`: controls while watching
- `/replay pause` and `/replay resume`: pause and continue
- `/replay speed <value>`: speed
- `/replay free`: free camera
- `/replay follow`: a player's view
- `/replay end`: end
