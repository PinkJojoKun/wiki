More about the mode under [SMP](/game-modes/smp).

## Territories

- `/camp`: open your camp
- `/camp add <player>`: add a resident
- `/city`: open your city
- `/city plots`: free plots
- `/city outpost`: outposts
- `/plot`: open your plot
- `/plot add <player>`: add a resident
- `/nation`: open your nation
- `/alliance`: open your alliance

For `/camp`, `/city`, `/plot`, `/nation` and `/alliance`:

- `view`: show borders
- `edit`: edit mode
- `off`: hide borders

## Death

- `/graves`: your graves

## Quests

- `/quests`: quests
- `/milestones`: milestones

## Trading

- `/ah` or `/auctionhouse`: auction house
- `/ah sell`: offer the item in your hand
- `/ah mailbox`: mailbox
- `/orders`: buy orders
- `/orders create`: create a buy order
- `/trade <player>`: request a trade
- `/trade`: open a running trade

## Spawners

- `/spawner`: spawner shop

## Skills

- `/skills`: abilities and mana
- `/skills tree`: skill tree
- `/skills toggle <ability>`: turn an ability on or off
- `/skills reset`: reset stars

## Other

- `/stats`: statistics
- `/credits`: credits
